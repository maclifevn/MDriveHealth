The bundled drivedb.json is converted from the included drivedb.h with
tools/convert_drivedb.py. Its defaults, entries and firmware fixes have
been checked against that source; the generated date is informational.
The macOS USB-bridge entries are excluded by the converter.

smartmontools os_darwin.h, with project-specific names and data structures.

reference for checking these declarations, not a claim about that revision.
